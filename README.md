# Spider-Man

My favorite superhero franchise is **Spider-Man**, following Peter Parker as he balances everyday life in New York City with his responsibilities as the web-slinging hero.

## Why It's Iconic
* **Relatable Hero:** Unlike billionaire or god-like heroes, Peter deals with real-world struggles like school, bills, and relationships.
* **Unforgettable Motto:** *"With great power comes great responsibility"* defines every version of his story.
* **Epic Multiverse & Action:** From Tobey Maguire, Andrew Garfield, and Tom Holland to Miles Morales in the *Spider-Verse* films, every era brings incredible action and heart.
